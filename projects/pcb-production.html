This is company work at RF-Frontend, so details are kept general for confidentiality. I'm happy to talk about it in more depth in an interview.

Overview

Alongside my software work, I have hands-on experience in PCB production: populating boards, hand-soldering, and testing, tuning and repairing finished PCBs.

PCB production

I operated an Autotronik pick-and-place (PnP) machine, which places surface-mount components on the board, and carried out multiple production runs from start to finish for different PCB layouts.

  • Operating the PnP machine:
    • Configuring it for different PCB layouts
    • Setting up component libraries for different footprints
    • Mounting feeders with components of different sizes
    • Fixing errors during the population process
  • Applying solder paste to the PCBs

Hand soldering

  • Soldering connectors and SMD components down to the 0201 package size (0.6 × 0.3 mm)

PCB testing & tuning

I have also worked on the individual testing and tuning of produced PCBs. Some of the tasks I performed include:

  • Oscilloscope-based inspection of waveforms measured on the PCB
  • Tuning of on-board potentiometers based on waveform measurements
  • Microscope-based inspection of faulty boards for missing or poorly soldered components, and repairing them
